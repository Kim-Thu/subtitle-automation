Troubleshooting & Help

Common Issues

1. Videos not showing up?

Ensure your video files are placed inside the inputs folder. Click the Refresh List button on the dashboard. The system supports .mp4, .mkv, .avi formats.

2. Ollama translation failed?

Make sure Ollama is installed and running on your machine. Run ollama serve in your terminal. Check if the model you selected (e.g. qwen:7b) is pulled via ollama list.

3. Error during "Burning Subtitles"?

This requires FFmpeg. Ensure FFmpeg is installed and added to your system PATH, or placed in the bin folder of the application.
